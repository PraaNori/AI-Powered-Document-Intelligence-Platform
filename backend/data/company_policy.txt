
## Company Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 20 paid leave days per year. Sick leave must be
reported before 10 AM on the day of absence. Unused leave can be carried
forward up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.
 
## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Remote workers must be available on company chat during core hours,
which are 10 AM to 4 PM local time.
 
## Expense Reimbursement
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
Claims above $500 require additional approval from the finance department.
Travel expenses must include original receipts.
 
## Onboarding Process
New employees complete a 5-day onboarding program covering company tools,
HR policies, and team introductions. IT equipment is provided on day one.
A buddy from the same team is assigned for the first 30 days.
